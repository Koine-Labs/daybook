RMSSD (the root mean square of successive RR-interval differences) is the primary
time-domain index of vagally mediated, beat-to-beat changes in heart rate variability.
Reductions in RMSSD accompany sympathetic activation and reduced parasympathetic
(vagal) tone. RMSSD norms vary substantially with age and individual baseline, so
absolute cut-points do not transfer across people. Short-window RMSSD is sensitive to
respiration rate, motion artifact, and posture.
